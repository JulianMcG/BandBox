1. Who can use BandBox

BandBox is built for band directors and the staff and volunteers who help them. You must be 18 or older, or the age of majority where you live, to create an account. If you're between 13 and 18 and helping run a program, you may use BandBox only with permission from a parent or guardian and from the staff member responsible for the program. Students don't need accounts and shouldn't be given one just to be tracked.

2. Your account

You sign in with an emailed six-digit code, with Apple, or with Google. Keep access to that email account and that device secure; anyone who can receive your codes can reach your inventory. Tell us at julianmcg08@gmail.com if you think someone has got into your account.

3. Bands and shared access

An inventory belongs to a band, not to a person. Anyone who enters your band's six-character join code becomes a member and can see and change the whole inventory: adding items, signing gear in and out, printing labels, and deleting items. Only the director who created the band can invite, remove members, or generate a new join code.

Two consequences worth stating plainly. Sharing your join code is sharing your inventory — treat it accordingly. And because the band belongs to its creator, if the creator deletes their account, the band and its entire inventory are deleted for everyone in it.

6. What BandBox is not

BandBox is a convenience tool for keeping track of school-owned instruments and uniforms. It is not an official record, an accounting system, an audit trail, or evidence for an insurance claim or a legal dispute. Specifically:

  • Syncing happens while the app is open, roughly every thirty seconds. It is not instantaneous.
  • If two people edit the same item at nearly the same moment, the later edit wins and the earlier one is lost. There is no merge and no warning.
  • Data on a single device can be lost like any other app data — a broken phone, a failed restore, a deletion.

Keep your own records of anything that matters financially or legally.
